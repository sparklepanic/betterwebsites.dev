hewoo,

This theme started off long ago as a simple set of html and css files that I manually edited to have basically a landing page with my links to other things.

Then it evolved into a full Hugo blog theme that I released in early 2025 which ran the site.

Until now.

The idea is simple: the designs of sites like https://en.wikipedia.org, https://craigslist.com, https://old.reddit.com, https://grapheneos.org, and https://text.npr.org/ are all cool and based (or in the words of one of my friends, “those sites fuck”).

I wanted to make a blog theme that’s simple, multi-author capable, was easy to read, and most importantly: that it fucks.

So, here is my humble and free offering, MIT licensed.

If anything about this theme is wrong or could be improved, lemme know by going to the site code and filing an issue or proposing a patch.

I wrote this page with a more normie person in mind, as I believe more folks should get comfortable taking back our means of computation. This is a static site and it can be deployed anywhere.

I encourage more of us to write.

— Raven
